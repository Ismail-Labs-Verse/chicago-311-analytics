Headline numbers

Read the median carefully. 58.4% of all records in this dataset were opened and closed by the 311 contact centre in under a minute — citizen information calls and auto-closed noise reports. They never involve dispatching a crew, so counting them makes the citywide median resolution time 5.75000 days (about 8280.0 minutes). Every response-time statistic in this dashboard is therefore computed over 1,742,542 actionable service requests, giving a median of 5.92 days. The administrative filings are kept for volume and seasonality analysis — they are still load on the system.
